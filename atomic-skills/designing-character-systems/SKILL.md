# Designing Character Systems

## Core principle

A recurring character is a **stateful system under pressure**, not a biography. Track what must remain recognizable, what may change, and what each relationship can generate.

## Character record

Keep:

- identity anchor: voice, values, signature behavior, visual/role markers;
- external wants and current goals;
- internal need / unresolved contradiction;
- wounds, fears, beliefs and self-deceptions;
- non-negotiable boundaries;
- capabilities, limits, costs and dependencies;
- public identity versus private truth;
- secrets held and secrets known;
- resources and obligations;
- current physical/emotional condition;
- affiliations and status;
- knowledge state;
- arc state;
- canon sources.

## Relationship graph

Relationships are directional. Track from A → B and B → A:

- trust;
- affection / attraction;
- fear;
- power;
- debt;
- loyalty;
- resentment;
- dependency;
- knowledge asymmetry;
- current unresolved question.

A useful ensemble contains **productive asymmetry**: people can cooperate on one axis and oppose each other on another.

## Arc contract

For each major character define:

- core promise that should remain recognizable;
- changeable traits;
- starting contradiction;
- pressure sequence;
- irreversible choices;
- consequences;
- endpoint for the current arc;
- what new conflict the endpoint creates.

Do not preserve characters by resetting them after every arc. Preserve identity while allowing state to change.

## Continuity snapshots

At every issue/episode boundary record:

- location;
- health;
- knowledge;
- relationships;
- possessions;
- allegiance;
- public reputation;
- active goals.

## Failure modes

- cloned motivations across the cast;
- abrupt moral or relational reversal without pressure and consequence;
- characters who know things they never learned;
- permanent trauma that disappears between installments;
- endless circular arcs;
- growth that removes the conflict engine;
- supporting characters who exist only as functions of the protagonist.

Pair with **planning-serialized-arcs** for long-form development and **auditing-lore-continuity** before handoffs.
