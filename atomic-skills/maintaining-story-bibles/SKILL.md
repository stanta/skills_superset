# Maintaining Story Bibles

## Core principle

A production bible is a **living navigation layer over structured records**, not one giant document that tries to contain every fact.

## Separate two products

Do not confuse:

- **pitch bible** — sells the premise, tone, characters, story engine and future potential;
- **production bible** — supports creators with current canon, state, chronology, rules and decisions.

A pitch bible can be concise and persuasive. A production bible must be searchable, versioned and operational.

## Production bible structure

Use linked modules:

1. premise, themes, tone and story engine;
2. world rules;
3. canon registry;
4. timeline / event ledger;
5. characters and relationship graph;
6. factions, locations and institutions;
7. powers / technology / artifacts;
8. active arcs and open-loop ledger;
9. terminology / naming rules;
10. continuity decisions and retcon log;
11. mysteries / deliberately withheld facts;
12. onboarding page for new contributors.

## Record provenance

Every important entry should show where it came from. Separate:

- established public canon;
- internal future plan;
- writer hypothesis;
- character belief;
- rejected idea.

## Update discipline

After each locked installment:

- ingest new canonical facts;
- update character snapshots;
- update relationships;
- add/close timeline events;
- close or advance open loops;
- record new rules/exceptions;
- run a continuity audit;
- log canon changes.

## Keep it usable

Prefer short linked records, tables and graphs over long prose. Use stable IDs and change history. Archive superseded facts rather than deleting them.

Do not over-document speculative lore. Capture uncertainty explicitly and promote it to canon only when approved or published.

## Handoff test

A new writer should be able to answer within minutes:

- what is true now?
- what is uncertain?
- what must not be contradicted?
- where is each claim sourced?
- what arcs are active?
- what does each major character know and want now?
- which mysteries are intentional?

Use the other narrative-worldbuilding skills as the detailed procedures behind each module.
