# Slide Writer Teaching

Convert an outline into final slide-ready content and presenter guidance.

## Use this skill when

Apply this skill when the user already has slide titles, a lecture outline, or draft slide text and needs a clean, teachable deck.

Typical triggers:
- "Write the slides"
- "Turn this outline into a deck"
- "Add speaker notes"
- "Make bullets shorter"
- "Suggest visuals for each slide"

## Do not use this skill when

Do not use this skill when the user has no outline and needs the entire lecture designed from scratch.

## Core workflow

1. Read the outline and determine the narrative arc.
2. Convert each slide into concise on-slide content:
   - title
   - up to 5 short bullets or another compact slide element suitable for the domain
   - visual suggestion
   - short speaker notes
3. Insert engagement cues every 2-3 slides where appropriate.
4. Produce support materials:
   - board/whiteboard plan
   - timing map
   - presenter notes tied to slide sequence
5. Adapt visual language to the domain:
   - STEM/technical: diagrams, formulas, code, process flows
   - humanities/social sciences: timelines, comparisons, conceptual maps, quotations
   - business/professional: frameworks, decision tables, process schemes
6. Keep slides minimalist and readable.

## Output requirements

Produce slide text that is presentation-ready, not essay-like. Keep slide density low and move explanation into speaker notes.

## Quality bar

Avoid paragraphs on slides, overloaded visuals, and disconnected slide order. Optimize for projection readability and classroom delivery.
